- **Player Character:** "The main character in a video game that the player controls. It's the central figure through which the player interacts with the game world."

- **Sprite:** Sprite is a 2D image used for visualizing game objects.

- **Obstacle:** "An element in a game that challenges the player, requiring skill, strategy, or quick thinking to overcome. Obstacles add difficulty and engagement to gameplay."

- **Dynamic Obstacle:** "An obstacle that moves or changes over time, requiring players to adapt their strategy and timing continuously."

- **Frame Rate:** "The frequency at which consecutive images (frames) appear on a display. Measured in frames per second (FPS), it determines how smooth motion appears in games and animations."
